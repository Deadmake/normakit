## SEO ground rules (from 2026 Etsy research)
- Title: 140 char max. Front-load the exact buyer phrase in the first ~40 chars (mobile
  truncates there). Readable, not keyword-spam.
- Tags: use all 13, each 20 char max, multi-word long-tail phrases, no repeats, do not
  duplicate title terms verbatim. Spend tags on synonyms, platforms (Shopify/Wix/WooCommerce),
  audience (small business, online seller), and Italian phrases (our low-competition edge).
- Attributes: set "Instant Download" / digital delivery and file-type metadata; fill every
  attribute (second query-match layer + buyer filters).
- Our real organic edge = bilingual EN + IT (Italian long-tails showed thin competition and
  no major competitor ships true bilingual) plus dual DOCX + PDF. Head terms ("privacy policy
  template") are for relevance only; we will not out-rank established shops there cold.
- CRITICAL for ads: Etsy Ads have NO manual keyword control. Etsy picks queries from our
  title/tags/attributes/description. So our SEO tags ARE our ad targeting. Fix listings first.

## P4: Etsy Ads plan (signal test, not revenue play)
Honest framing from research: EUR 20 to 30 is a signal-detection budget. At ~0.30 to 0.50
USD/click expect ~45 to 90 total clicks over the run. Cold, unreviewed shops commonly get
many impressions and ZERO sales, so 0 to a few sales is the realistic range. We are buying a
CONVERSION SIGNAL (does a real buyer click and cart/buy?), not profit.

2026 refinement (important): the strong consensus is to NOT advertise a listing with zero
reviews/sales. New listings get a free organic "honeymoon" boost. Higher-EV sequence:
1. Publish all 3 listings with dialed-in titles/tags/images/price. Let them run organically
   through the honeymoon window (a few days) to gather CTR/favorites and ideally a first
   review or two.
2. THEN switch on Etsy Ads as the paid signal test.

Ads config when we switch on:
- Budget: ~5 USD/day (~EUR 4.50) for ~5 days = ~25 USD (~EUR 22 to 23). Clears Etsy's own
  3 to 5 USD/day test threshold; enough clicks to be readable. Well within EUR 75 remaining
  and EUR 100 MISSION cap. LOG the spend in LEDGER.md before enabling.
- Promote: the Full Pack (Listing 3, highest price + intent + margin) as primary, plus the
  Privacy Policy (Listing 1) as the strongest single. Do NOT thinly spread across all 3.
- No keyword picking (automatic). Our tags do the targeting, so tags above are the ad strategy.
- Measure daily: impressions, clicks, CTR, add-to-cart, sales, spend. Hold to KC-E: within
  5 days of shop live + EUR 20-30 ads, if >= 300 high-intent impressions but < 1 sale AND
  < 3 add-to-carts, the cold EUR offer is the ceiling -> change the OFFER (cheaper single-doc
  lead magnet or free-doc + paid upgrade), not just copy.

## Pricing / competition note
Live 2026 band: single legal-template listings ~USD 3 to 30 (credible pro-written ones sit
~10 to 30); bundles ~USD 29 to 50. Our EUR 7 / 9 / 22 sits at the credible-but-affordable
part of the band. Do not price to USD 2 to 4 (signals "cheap generic Canva" for a legal doc).
Bundle is priced clearly below the sum of singles (anchoring). If we run a "sale," it must be
a genuine reduction from a real prior price (Etsy truthful-advertising policy). Never promise
guaranteed compliance; frame as "helps you build a GDPR-aligned policy." Disclaimer is
mandatory and also protects the Partita IVA under EU consumer law.
